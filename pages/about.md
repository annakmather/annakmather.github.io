# **About Me**

Hi there! I’m Anna, a Chicago based data engineer and analytics engineer (and occassional data scientist). Currently, I work as a data engineer for SRAM, a bicycle components company. In the past I’ve worked as a consultant, helping progressive political and non profit organizations tackle their data problems. 

Outside of work, I’m a passionate cyclist, focusing on distance and gravel events. I ride for, and am a founding member of The Dropettes. When I’m not out exploring midwestern roads, I can be found knitting and taking the occassional guitar class.
                      
I’d love to hear from you! Feel free to reach out via email.
